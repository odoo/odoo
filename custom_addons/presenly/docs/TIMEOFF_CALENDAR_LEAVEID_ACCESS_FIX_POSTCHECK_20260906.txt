PURPOSE
- Fix "You do not have enough rights to access the field leave_id on Time Off
  Calendar" for users who have Presenly Approver but not the native
  hr_holidays.group_hr_holidays_user group.

ROOT CAUSE
- Native field `hr.leave.report.calendar.leave_id` is declared with
  `groups='hr_holidays.group_hr_holidays_user'`.
- Yusril has `hr_holidays.group_hr_holidays_responsible` (Time Off
  Responsible), not `group_hr_holidays_user`.
- The Presenly related/computed fields on the calendar view read `leave_id`
  without sudo, triggering the native field access error.

FIX
- In presenly_report_calendar.py:
  - `presenly_approval_state` and `presenly_approval_progress` now use
    `related_sudo=True`.
  - `_compute_presenly_calendar_permissions` reads `record.sudo().leave_id`.
  - `action_presenly_approve` and `action_presenly_open_reject_wizard` read the
    leave id via `self.sudo().leave_id.id` and act through the leave recordset.
- No native group was granted to Yusril; the native field restriction stays
  intact, but Presenly controls only use sudo to reach the related leave.
