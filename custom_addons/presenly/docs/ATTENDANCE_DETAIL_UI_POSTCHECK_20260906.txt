EMPLOYEE SELECTOR ROOT CAUSE AND FIX
- Native Odoo makes Employee selectable for users in hr_attendance.group_hr_attendance_user.
- The two Employee accounts had this group assigned directly, outside the Presenly role hierarchy.
- Upgrade 19.0.9.0.0 removes that direct cross-employee management grant only from Presenly Employee/Approver users who are not Presenly HR.
- The cleanup is idempotent.
- Presenly HR now officially implies native Officer: Manage all attendances.
- Presenly Administrator retains HR and native Attendance management through the role hierarchy.
- Employee/Approver users receive a dedicated Attendances > Overview > My Attendances action.
- My Attendances has domain [('employee_id.user_id', '=', uid)] and disables create/delete.
- Employee is rendered as read-only with no create/open interaction for employee self-service.

SERVER-SIDE SECURITY
- UI locking is not the only protection.
- HrAttendance.create rejects an Employee/Approver creating attendance for another Employee.
- HrAttendance.write rejects an Employee/Approver changing employee_id.
- Native is_manager is forced false for Presenly Employee/Approver users who are not HR, preventing timestamp editing inherited from accidental native groups.
- Presenly HR/Administrator may still perform corrections subject to native Odoo Attendance Manager/Administrator authorization.
- Employee identity for mobile API remains derived from the authenticated session.
