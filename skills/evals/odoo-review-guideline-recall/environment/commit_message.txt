[ADD] equipment_booking: book shared equipment

Teams share projectors, vans and measuring tools, and today they book
them in a spreadsheet: two people regularly show up for the same van,
and customers who rent equipment have to call to know when it is free.

This module lets employees and portal customers book a bookable piece
of equipment for a period. Bookings of the same equipment cannot
overlap, the holder gets a reminder the day before, and a label with a
check-in code is printed for each booking. Cancelling a booking
notifies the equipment's technician, so the slot can be offered again.
A booking board shows, per piece of equipment, the bookings confirmed
or in use and how many are late, so the desk sees what goes out.

Maintenance equipment gets a "Booking Holder" assignment: the owner of
a booked piece of equipment is the holder of its current booking. To
compute it, the owner methods now receive the date. The serial number
field is renamed serial_number, the name the booking labels and the
portal use.
