[FIX] l10n_it_stock_ddt: crash when renaming a warehouse sequence

Steps to reproduce:
- In an Italian company, open the Delivery Orders operation type of a
  warehouse
- Change its sequence prefix and its warehouse in the same write

A KeyError: 'warehouse_ids' is raised: write() reads the wrong key when
the warehouse is part of the values, so the DDT sequence cannot be
renamed, and test_ddt_sequence_follows_warehouse fails on the l10n
build.

Master fixes the key in 9e2d7c4b1a6f and, while at it, passes the
warehouse record to _get_dtt_ir_seq_vals instead of its id. Only the
key is fixed here: on a released version the method keeps its
signature, extension modules may override it or call it with an id.
The test is adapted to call it as 20.0 defines it.
