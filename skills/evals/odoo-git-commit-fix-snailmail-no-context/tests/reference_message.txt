[FIX] snailmail: avoid duplicate zip/city on German cover

Steps to reproduce:
- Enable the snailmail cover page on the company
- Send a letter by post to a German partner having a Street 2
- Open the generated document

Issue:
The zip/city line is printed twice on the cover page, so Pingen
rejects the letter as it does not meet the Deutsche Post address
requirements.

Cause:
For German addresses, the cover address lines are adapted by
overwriting fixed positions of the partner's address lines. When a
Street 2 is set, the street line is merged with Street 2 but the
former Street 2 line is overwritten with zip/city instead of being
removed, leaving the original zip/city line in place.

Build the German cover address explicitly from the letter address
instead of patching line positions.
