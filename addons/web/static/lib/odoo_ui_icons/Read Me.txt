The fonts in `fonts/` are generated -- do not edit them by hand.  They are built
by `odoo/addons/web/tooling/icons/generate_icons.py` out of the FontAwesome 4.7
glyphs listed in `tooling/icons/fa_icons_wishlist.txt` and the SVGs in
`tooling/icons/custom_icons/`.  FontAwesome codepoints come from its upstream
stylesheet; custom SVG codepoints are listed in
`tooling/icons/custom_icons_wishlist.json`.

Versions up to 1.2 were created by SRI using IcoMoon (https://icomoon.io/).
